MANDATORY POWER OF TEN REQUIREMENT

Operator clarification, 2026-10-21: Gerard J. Holzmann's NASA/JPL "The Power of Ten - Rules for Developing Safety-Critical Code" is a binding engineering and completion requirement for SIA, not optional guidance or a reading prerequisite. The operator explicitly authorizes necessary implementation, architectural and refactoring changes to make the system safer and more reliable. This does not waive existing security, compatibility, evidence, migration or release gates.

Retain the original ten rules and rationales as the normative source; preserve them verbatim when quoting. Identify cross-language controls as adaptations, never as Holzmann's words. Use the existing docs/POWER-OF-TEN-ASSESSMENT.md and applicable Ada/SPARK guidance to maintain a rule-by-rule map covering production code, adapters, scripts, tests, libraries and experimental code. Size does not exempt code. Include third-party/runtime limitations explicitly rather than implying whole-system compliance from a proved core.

Require analyzable control flow; bounded loops and explicit service-lifecycle arguments; budgeted resources; short single-purpose routines; meaningful contracts/assertions and failure actions; narrow scope and authority; checked inputs, outputs and errors; transparent semantics; restricted aliasing/indirection; and strict warnings/static analysis enforced in CI. These are the operator's cross-language controls, not quotations of the original rules. Keep consequential authorization in the verified Ada/SPARK core and qualify its actual enforcement and integration.

For each original rule and its language-specific controls, the acceptance manifest must identify the exact source scope, enforcement/check command, result, remaining violations and supporting proof/test/review evidence. An inventory or a statement that the file was read cannot establish compliance. Mechanically enforce controls where feasible and retain manual verification obligations where tools cannot establish them. Repair violations; any deviation requires written necessity, risk assessment, compensating controls and explicit operator approval before acceptance. No deviation is approved by this clarification.

Completion is blocked by unresolved applicable Power of Ten violations or missing compliance evidence. Preserve PROVED, TESTED, OBSERVED, ASSUMED, UNPROVEN and FAILED distinctions. The final acceptance report must include this rule-by-rule assessment alongside A01-A24, G1-G6 and C01-C14.
