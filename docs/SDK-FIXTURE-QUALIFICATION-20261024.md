## Limits and next acceptance evidence

The successful fixed-guard operations and builtin bindings are trusted.
Arbitrary replacement of those bindings, frame/bytecode introspection,
interruption between advancing an index and attempting its retirement, and
hostile concurrent fixture mutation are not qualified. Exhaustive attempts
cannot guarantee an empty earlier slot if a finalizer repopulates it later.
Changed traceback shape still requires the complete original diagnostic and
output controls. Python allocation is not formally bounded by this work.

The next step is to diagnose the remaining complete-suite cost on this exact
successor before another full attempt. No unchanged retry, cap increase,
test deduplication, shared mutable harness or skipped clear establishes a pass.
Acceptance still requires the entire original suite within its original
envelope, followed by separate prospective SDK/native and installed-runtime
qualification. C analysis, SPARK proof, retained-validation R05, continuity,
recovery, OS enforcement and release obligations remain open.
